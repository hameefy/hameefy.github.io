Numerical Optimization Digest

A highly selective weekly digest with two independent sections: up to seven peer-reviewed research articles from Springer Nature, Elsevier, Taylor & Francis, SIAM, and Wiley, followed by a curated arXiv preprint shortlist. Priority topics include unconstrained optimization, gradient and conjugate-gradient methods, quasi-Newton methods, line search, global and convergence-rate analysis, nonconvex optimization, nonlinear equations and nonlinear least squares, monotone operator equations, logistic regression, machine-learning optimization, and signal processing. These topics guide ranking rather than act as hard exclusions. Each issue reports main result claims and what to read first.
