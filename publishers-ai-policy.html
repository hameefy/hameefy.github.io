Publishers AI Policy Decks

Slide decks summarising how major publishers expect authors, reviewers and editors to use AI tools. They were prepared with Claude from the publishers' public policy pages (accessed October 2026) and include speaker notes, practice scenarios tailored to numerical optimisation, and checklists. The decks paraphrase the policies and are not legal advice or official publisher text: always check the current policy and your journal's own instructions before submitting or reviewing.
